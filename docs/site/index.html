SpamHole downloads publisher datasets for call reputation, then matches callers on your device. Your personal allow and block rules stay local.

Evidence has limits

FTC unwanted-call complaints support identification, not automatic blocking. Complaints are unverified, and displayed numbers can be spoofed. A reputation association index is a heuristic, not a probability or proof of who originated a call.

Custom subscriptions cannot grant themselves authority to block calls. Personal allow rules take priority over SpamHole’s other decisions.
